Industry Challenge Harbour · planned

Put the learning to work.

Planned industry challenges and buildathons would give ready participants a place to collaborate on real problems and demonstrate their solutions.

How a challenge would work.

What a company contributes
  • A scoped, real use case
  • Clear constraints
  • Permissioned or synthetic data
  • Feedback on what participants build
What participants show
  • A working prototype, built together with mentoring
  • The evidence behind their choices
  • The limits of what they built

An optional learning opportunity, not a third compulsory route, guaranteed work, accreditation or automatic teaching approval. Its place in assessment and recognition is still to be agreed.

For industry partners

Bring a bounded problem.

Bring a bounded problem that participants can explore, prototype and learn from, with clear expectations around data, mentoring and what a prototype can demonstrate. It could suit a workshop, an applied project or a buildathon.
