Before you ask

Is Beaver Guild a course provider or a community?

Both. Beaver Guild brings together a community and structured learning: Tutorial Island as the shared start, then two pathways, practitioner and trainer. The trainer pathway is optional. Dates, pricing and access details will be shared before enrolment.

Will I receive a certificate?

Recognition arrangements are not yet finalised. Any future completion record, assessed practitioner achievement and guild trainer approval will be clearly distinguished. No official Notion trainer certification or external accreditation is being promised.

Are teaching opportunities or jobs guaranteed?

No. The guild aims to develop capability and create opportunities, but participation is not a guarantee of paid work, placement or income.

Is this an official Notion programme?

No. Beaver Guild is an independent initiative.
